LOCATION: ROME\nDATE: A.D. 1486\nOBJECTIVE: ESCAPE







BENE, BROTHER! THE ASSASSINS ARE YOUR ALLIES NOW. BUT BORGIA'S MEN ARE HUNTING FOR YOU AS WE SPEAK. YOU MUST NOT BE CAPTURED. GO!
IF I SET THOSE CRATES ON FIRE AND FLY OVER THEM, THE HEAT DRAFT SHOULD LET ME CLIMB HIGHER!
I MUST REACH A BONFIRE!
THEY'RE FLYING BORGIA'S COLORS! I'LL DROP FLAMING PITCH ON THEIR HEADS!
KEEP TOUCHING THE ASSASSIN OR THE ATTACK ICON AND RELEASE WHEN YOU'VE FINISHED CHARGING FOR A BIGGER EXPLOSION!
